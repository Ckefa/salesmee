Font Awesome Kit Icons for SalesMee
====================================
Create a kit at https://kit.fontawesome.com and add these 116 icons.

After creating the kit and copying your kit ID (looks like abc123def), run:
  sed -i 's|cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/font-awesome/6.4.0/css/all.min.css|kit.fontawesome.com/YOUR_KIT_ID.js|' web/templates/**/*.html web/templates/**/**/*.html
  sed -i 's|<link|<script src="https://kit.fontawesome.com/YOUR_KIT_ID.js" crossorigin="anonymous"></script>|' web/templates/**/*.html web/templates/**/**/*.html
  (Then remove the old <link> line that remains)

Or simpler: just replace YOUR_KIT_ID with your actual ID everywhere:
  sed -i 's|YOUR_KIT_ID|your-actual-kit-id|g' web/templates/**/*.html web/templates/**/**/*.html
