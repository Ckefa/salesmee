Getting Started

Set up your business in minutes

Create Your Account

Sign up with your email or Google account. Choose your business type, add your business name, logo, and contact details. Your public profile is generated automatically.

Customize Your Profile

Add a cover image, write a compelling description, set your business hours, and configure your address. Your public profile at /b/your-slug goes live instantly.

Pro Tip

Use a high-quality logo and write a clear business description. Businesses with complete profiles get 3x more client engagement.

2

Products & Services

List what you offer

Add Products or Services

Navigate to your dashboard and open Products or Services. Add a name, description, price, and optional image. You can organize items into categories and set availability windows for services.

  • Set custom pricing (fixed or range)
  • Toggle availability on/off individually
  • Group items by category for easy browsing
  • Services support duration and buffer time
3

Managing Orders

Receive, track, and fulfill customer orders

Order Lifecycle

Orders flow through statuses: Pending → Confirmed → Completed or Cancelled. Update status from the dashboard or use the Quick Order modal for instant creation.

Quick Order Modal

Click the Quick Order floating button on your dashboard to create an order in seconds. Select a client, pick products, set the status — all without leaving your current view.

4

Bookings & Appointments

Accept and manage client appointments

How Bookings Work

Clients can book your services directly from your public profile. Each service has a defined duration and price. You can also create bookings manually from the dashboard using the Quick Booking modal. All bookings sync to your dashboard in real-time.

Duration-based Real-time notifications Auto-sync calendar
5

Client Communication

Real-time chat with your clients

Live Chat

Each client connection opens a dedicated chat channel. Messages deliver instantly — no refresh needed. Share order confirmations, booking details, and answer questions in real-time.

Share Your Widget

Generate an embeddable chat widget from your dashboard. Add it to your website so clients can reach you directly. The widget shows your business name, status, and opens a chat instantly.

6

Analytics & Insights

Track your business performance

Dashboard Analytics

Your analytics dashboard gives you a bird's-eye view of your business. Monitor total orders, revenue, booking rates, and client growth. Use these insights to identify your most popular services and peak hours.

Connecting to a Business

Get started in seconds

Via Business Profile

Visit any business's public profile at /b/slug and click Connect. Enter your name and phone number or email, verify with a one-time code, and you're connected.

Via Widget

Businesses can embed a chat widget on their website. Click the chat icon, enter your details, and you'll be connected instantly. No app download required.

Privacy First

Your contact information is only shared with businesses you explicitly connect to. You control who can reach you.

2

Placing Orders

Order products and services seamlessly

How to Order

Once connected to a business, browse their products and services from their profile. Select what you need and submit your order. The business gets notified instantly and will confirm or update the status. You'll receive real-time updates through the chat.

Instant notification Real-time status Chat updates
3

Booking Services

Schedule appointments with ease

Book from Profile

Visit the business profile, browse their services, pick a date and time, and confirm. The booking appears instantly on both your and the business's calendar.

Duration & Availability

Each service shows its duration so you know how much time to set aside. Businesses define their availability, so you'll only see bookable slots that work for both of you.

4

Live Chat

Talk to businesses in real-time

Chat Features

Every connection opens a dedicated chat channel. Messages arrive instantly — no page refresh needed. Use chat to ask questions about products, confirm booking details, or get support. Your conversation history is always available.

Real-time delivery Chat history Push notifications
5

Managing Your Account

Stay in control

Edit Your Profile

Update your name, email, or phone number from the client portal. Businesses you're connected to will see your updated info automatically.

Manage Connections

View all businesses you're connected to and disconnect at any time. Disconnecting removes the business's access to your information.

Delete Your Data

Request account deletion at any time via the User Deletion page. Your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Is there a mobile app?
OneFlow is fully responsive and works great on mobile browsers. A native mobile app is on our roadmap — stay tuned!
Can I connect with multiple businesses?
Absolutely. As a client, you can connect to as many businesses as you'd like. Each connection has its own chat channel and order history.
How much does OneFlow cost?
OneFlow offers a free tier for businesses with essential features. Premium plans unlock advanced analytics, unlimited products, custom branding, and priority support. Check the Pricing section for details.
Is my data secure?
Yes. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. Your personal information is never shared without your explicit consent. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I cancel or modify an order?
Yes. Contact the business through the chat to request changes or cancellation. Businesses can update order status from their dashboard.
